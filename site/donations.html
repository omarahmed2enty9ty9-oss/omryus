Donations

Last updated 20 September 2026

Some partner codes carry no discount — they only track the sale. Rather than pass those off as savings, Omryus offers them as donations. If you choose to add one, 100% of the commission goes to Medical Aid for Palestinians and we keep none of it.

Commission is paid to us first and forwarded by us — the shop does not send it directly. That makes it a promise rather than a mechanism, which is why this page exists: every payment is listed below with its date and amount, so the promise can be checked rather than trusted.

How this is calculated

  • Only commission from donation-funded codes is counted here — codes that gave the shopper no discount.
  • Commission from ordinary discount codes is our revenue and is not included. The extension says which kind of code it is offering, every time.
  • The amount sent is the commission actually received, before our own costs. We do not deduct anything.
  • Where a merchant reverses a commission (a returned order, say), the reversal is carried into the next period rather than reclaimed from the charity.

Questions about this

If a figure here looks wrong, or you want to see the underlying affiliate statement, ask — support@omryus.example. A donation claim nobody can check is not worth making.
