Donations

Last updated 20 September 2026

Some partner codes carry no discount — they only track the sale. Rather than pass those off as savings, Omryus offers them as donations. If you choose to add one, the whole commission goes to charity and when there isn't a discount, we keep none of it.

We're currently looking for a charity partner. Naming one in our marketing needs their written agreement first, and we don't have it yet — so we're not going to put a name here we can't stand behind. We'll name them the moment it's signed, and every payment below will say exactly who received it.

Commission from codes that do discount something is ordinary revenue and is how Omryus stays free. It is not counted here, and this page never claims otherwise.

Commission is paid to us first and forwarded by us — the shop does not send it directly. That makes it a promise rather than a mechanism, which is why this page exists: every payment is listed below with its date, amount and recipient, so the promise can be checked rather than trusted.

How this is calculated

  • Only commission from donation-funded codes is counted here — codes that gave the shopper no discount.
  • Commission from ordinary discount codes is our revenue and is not included. The extension says which kind of code it is offering, every time.
  • The amount sent is the commission actually received, before our own costs. We do not deduct anything.
  • Where a merchant reverses a commission (a returned order, say), the reversal is carried into the next period rather than reclaimed from the charity.

Questions about this

If a figure here looks wrong, or you want to see the underlying affiliate statement, ask — support@omryus.com. A donation claim nobody can check is not worth making.
