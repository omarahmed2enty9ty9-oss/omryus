Privacy policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

The short version

Omryus has no server, no accounts and no analytics service. Nothing about the pages you visit leaves your browser. There is no data for us to sell, lose, or be compelled to hand over.

What the extension can access

Omryus requests access only to the specific store domains we currently have a discount code for. These are listed on the extension's Chrome Web Store page and are visible in its manifest.json. On those stores — and only on basket and checkout pages — the extension checks whether a discount box exists, so it can put a code in it when you ask.

On every other website, the extension is not loaded at all. Not idle in the background, not watching: absent.

What it never accesses

  • Payment card, bank or billing details.
  • Passwords. The extension explicitly refuses to write into password or payment fields, even if a stored selector happens to match one.
  • Anything you type into a form.
  • Your basket contents, order history or browsing history.

What is stored, and where

In your browser's local extension storage, on your device only:

  • Your settings.
  • Four counters — how many times an offer was shown, applied or dismissed, and whether the extension was installed. These are numbers with no site or page attached, they are never transmitted, and you can switch them off or clear them in Settings.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

Affiliate disclosure

Where a partner programme allows it, using a discount code Omryus provided supports us — at no extra cost to you. This only ever happens after you click the button. We do not redirect you, rewrite links, or set tracking cookies, and we never replace a referral or affiliate attribution that already belongs to somebody else.

Some partner codes carry no discount at all. We will never present one of those as a saving. We offer them only as donations: if you choose to add one, we donate 100% of the commission to Medical Aid for Palestinians and keep none of it. Commission reaches our account first and is forwarded by us, so this is a promise about our conduct — and we publish what was sent.

Omryus currently has no partner agreements. The codes in the extension today are clearly-labelled test data.

Changes

If we ever start collecting data that leaves your device, we will update this page, update the Chrome Web Store listing, and tell you inside the extension before it happens.
