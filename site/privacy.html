Omryus

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version

Omryus has no server, no accounts and no analytics service. Nothing about the pages you visit leaves your browser. There is no data for us to sell, lose or hand over.

What the extension can access

Omryus requests access only to the specific store domains we currently have a discount code for. These are listed in the extension's Chrome Web Store entry and are visible in manifest.json. On those stores, and only on cart and checkout pages, the extension checks whether a discount box exists so it can put a code in it when you ask.

What it never accesses

  • Payment card, bank or billing details.
  • Passwords. The extension explicitly refuses to write into password or payment fields.
  • Anything you type into a form.
  • Your basket contents, order history or browsing history.
  • Any page on any site we don't have an offer for. The extension does not run there at all.

What is stored, and where

In your browser's local extension storage, on your device only:

  • Your settings.
  • Four counters — how many times an offer was shown, applied or dismissed, and whether the extension was installed. These are numbers only, with no site or page attached, and they are never transmitted. You can switch them off or clear them in Settings.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

Affiliate disclosure

Where a partner programme allows it, using a code Omryus provided supports us — at no extra cost to you. This only happens after you click Apply discount. We do not redirect you, rewrite links, or set tracking cookies, and we never replace a referral or affiliate attribution that already belongs to somebody else.

We will never show you a code that discounts nothing. An affiliate code that earns us money but saves you none is not something we are willing to put in your basket.

Omryus currently has no partner agreements. The codes in the extension today are clearly-labelled mock data used for testing.

Changes

If we ever start collecting data that leaves your device, we will update this page, update the Chrome Web Store listing, and tell you inside the extension before it happens.
