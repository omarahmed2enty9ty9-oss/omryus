What Omryus can see

  • The address of pages on stores we have an offer for — and no others.
  • Whether a discount box exists on that page.
  • Nothing else. We never read what you type, your basket, your card details or your password.
  • No page content ever leaves your browser. Omryus has no server.

Affiliate disclosure

Omryus inserts a discount code only after you click Apply discount. Where a partner programme allows it, using the code supports us — at no extra cost to you. We never overwrite a referral or affiliate attribution that already belongs to somebody else.

Some partner codes carry no discount at all. We will never present one of those as a saving. We offer them only as donations: if you choose to add one, the whole commission goes to the charity named below and we keep none of it. A code that earns us money and saves you nothing is not something we are willing to put in your basket, and Chrome Web Store policy forbids it too.

To be plain about the rest: when a code does save you money, we do keep that commission. That is how Omryus stays free. The donation applies specifically to codes that gave you nothing.

Donations are forwarded by us, not sent automatically by the store — commission reaches our account first. We publish what was sent.

The codes shipped today are clearly-labelled mock data. We have no real partnerships yet.
