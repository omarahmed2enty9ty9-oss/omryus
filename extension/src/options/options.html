Local counters

What Omryus can see

  • The address of pages on stores we have an offer for — and no others.
  • Whether a discount box exists on that page.
  • Nothing else. We never read what you type, your basket, your card details or your password.
  • No page content ever leaves your browser. Omryus has no server.

Affiliate disclosure

Omryus inserts a discount code only after you click Apply discount. Where a partner programme allows it, using the code supports us — at no extra cost to you. We never overwrite a referral or affiliate attribution that already belongs to somebody else.

We will never show you a code that discounts nothing. An affiliate code that earns us money but saves you none is not something we are willing to put in your basket, and Chrome Web Store policy forbids it too.

The codes shipped today are clearly-labelled mock data. We have no real partnerships yet.
